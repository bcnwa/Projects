README Summary: Customer Marketing & Campaign Analytics Project
Project Overview

This analysis focuses on understanding customer demographics, campaign effectiveness, purchasing behaviour, channel performance, customer loyalty, and strategic growth opportunities using a marketing campaign dataset. The objective is to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights through exploratory analysis, feature engineering, segmentation, correlation analysis, and performance measurement.

Business Objectives

The project aims to answer six key business areas:

Customer Demographics & Segmentation
Campaign Effectiveness
Product Purchase Behaviour
Channel & Interaction Analysis
Customer Loyalty & Retention
Strategic Business Insights
Feature Engineering

To enhance analytical capability, several new features should be created.

1. Total Product Spend

Combines customer spending across all product categories.

=MntWines+MntFruits+MntMeatProducts+MntFishProducts+MntSweetProducts+MntGoldProds

Purpose
Measure customer lifetime product expenditure.
Segment customers into Low, Medium, and High spenders.
Support loyalty and upselling analysis.
2. Campaign Engagement Score

Measures overall customer interaction with marketing campaigns.

=AcceptedCmp1+AcceptedCmp2+AcceptedCmp3+AcceptedCmp4+AcceptedCmp5+Response

Purpose
Identify highly engaged customers.
Measure campaign responsiveness.
Improve audience targeting.
3. Complaint Response Indicator

Combines complaints and campaign responses.

=Complain+Response

Purpose
Identify customers who respond despite making complaints.
Detect customers at risk of churn.
4. Family Size

Feature requested to combine marital status and household composition.

Formula
=IF(Marital_Status="Single",1,
IF(Marital_Status="Widow",1,
IF(Marital_Status="Divorced",1,2)))+Kidhome+Teenhome

Purpose
Measure household size.
Analyse spending patterns by family composition.
Understand product preferences across household groups.
5. Income Group

Segment customers into income bands.

Example:

Low Income
Middle Income
High Income

Purpose
Compare buying behaviours.
Evaluate campaign acceptance.
Support targeting strategies.
